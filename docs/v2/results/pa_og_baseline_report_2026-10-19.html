Planner-assisted baseline: first reproducible full-task successes

Label: PA-OG, planner-assisted with an oracle goal. This is not an end-to-end result. The planner's navigation goals use privileged simulator state; the π0.5 policy's inputs, its 18-D action interface and every phase switch use observable signals only. Policy v2-B (sha256 405f2d5d…), fp32, H_exec 5, unless marked M-SFT v1.

Outcome
  • Non-zero full-task success, verified by replay: 40/41 recorded successes re-execute from a fresh reset to ovmm_place_success = True (16 distinct episodes). The v2 end-to-end evals had 0 successes. Not reproduced: 516#0 (pa_msft_val; replay ends with place = False), a marginal placement; it is not counted as a verified success.
  • v2-B, PA-OG + base lock: train units 15/60 (25%, 3 seeds), held-out episodes 3/40, OVMM-val 0/30. Generalization beyond the training scenes is the open problem.
  • Manipulation works once navigation is solved, but only partially. From the expert's arrival, π0.5 places successfully in 46% of trials; grasp-to-carry-ready (24%) is the main bottleneck. The v2 zero-success was navigation.
  • Manipulation-only fine-tune (M-SFT v1, 3000 steps from v2-B): PA-OG + base lock, train units 15/40 vs v2-B 10/40 (same seeds), held-out 1/20 vs 2/20, OVMM-val 1/30 vs 0/30.

Closed-loop determinism depends on serving. With the shared, batched fp32 servers used for every pass above, re-running a success with the same unit and noise seed reproduced it in 3 of 7 re-runs. The others diverged within the first manipulation phase. With a dedicated --max-batch 1 server, two closed-loop runs of 17858#0 were bitwise identical (846/846 executed joint actions, max difference 0.0; that episode failed both times). So batch composition is the source, and unbatched serving makes rollouts exactly re-executable. Rates across seeds and episodes are the policy-level reproducibility measure; open-loop replay is the episode-level one. Two seed-0 successes recorded before joint logging existed (17858#0, 14956#0) are not replayable.

5 · Controller and observation contract (PA-OG + base lock)

Policy inputs (unchanged from v2-B training)camera_top ← head_rgb, camera_left ← right_wrist_rgb, camera_right ← left_wrist_rgb (240×320), camera_front masked; 16-D state (checkpoint convention); head history at −5/−10/−15 steps; instruction “Move the {object} from the {start} to the {goal}.”
Policy outputCartesianMobileAction18 chunk (H_pred 50), executed receding-horizon with H_exec 5 through the expert's CartesianExecutor (IK), one atomic 18-D joint action per 10 Hz step. Base lock: dims 16–17 (v, ω) replaced by 0 during manipulation; raw values traced.
Privileged, planner only (oracle goal)Target object pose, goal-receptacle surfaces, navmesh, IK feasibility: the expert's standoff planner (pick arm by geodesic cost, place sample). Never sent to the policy and never used in a switch.
Observable switchesHolding = a gripper commanded closed (≥ 0.5) whose measured closure stops more than 0.05 short of the command. Tucked = both arms within 0.3 rad of the NAV tuck. Arrival = planner odometry vs its own waypoint. Each condition must hold for 3 consecutive steps.
  1. NAV1, planner: arms to the NAV tuck, shortest navmesh path to the pick standoff, turn to its heading. Switch on arrival.
  2. MANIP1, π0.5: until holding ∧ tucked (timeout 600 steps).
  3. NAV2, planner: arms to the exact tuck with grippers held, place standoff planned from the object as actually held. Switch on arrival.
  4. MANIP2, π0.5: until ¬holding ∧ tucked (timeout 500 steps).
  5. Settle: hold still 120 steps (fixed, no object-velocity check), then read OVMM's measures.
